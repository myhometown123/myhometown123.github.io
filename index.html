Your hometown, found for you.

Enter your ZIP code. A master agent convenes five sub-agents every morning at 8:00 AM — they find the locations, contacts, events, history, and vacation spots worth knowing, and build a page for your city.

Try: Springfield, MO Branson, MO Kansas City, MO

🗓️ Daily 8 AM meeting

The master agent convenes the team every day at 8:00 AM, hands out briefs, and signs off on the combined find. Add ?meeting=yes to any city URL to convene it on demand.

🤖 Five sub-agents

Locations Scout, Contacts Finder, Events Watcher, History Researcher, and Vacation Planner each bring their specialty to the board.

🛠️ Admin control

Enable or pause agents, review the meeting log, and tune the schedule on the admin page.

Questions or corrections? Write to ckonkol@gmail.com. This is a demo build — findings are generated locally in your browser and never leave it.
